# Brand — CHANGEME: what may and may not be said

Everything the agent sends reaches a real person from a public brand account.
These rules override throughput, always.

## What this product is

Example of why this matters: document-preparation software for a regulated
process must never read as the regulated service itself, so its rules forbid
anything that sounds like representation or advice. Your product's equivalent
boundary goes here. If it is regulated -- health, finance, legal, insurance,
employment -- name the boundary explicitly.

## When unsure

Send a safe redirect to the site rather than improvising. Hostile, ambiguous or
complex replies get the redirect. Staying general is always allowed; guessing
is not.

## Verification before you record anything

A phone report claiming success is not success. Log an action only when the
operator's report contains the ground-truth check the app playbook demands --
for LinkedIn that means a name present in Sent invitations, or a sent bubble
confirmed on a fresh re-open of the thread; for X, the reply visible under
the post on a fresh open. Unverified means not logged.
