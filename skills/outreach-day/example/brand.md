# Brand — example agent (fictional): what may and may not be said

## What this product is

A code-review routing tool. It does not write, approve or merge code, and it
makes no claims about code quality or security. Never imply it does.

## Facts you may state

Only what `agent.yaml` `offer:` contains: the site, the plan prices and
that they are per seat per month. Render messages with
`outreach.ts message --name warmup` rather than typing prices.

## Never

- No numbers that are not on the site: no "cuts review time by X%".
- No claims about security, compliance certifications or customers by name.
- No links, prices or "DM me" in a public reply.
- Nothing that reads as advice on someone's specific codebase or team.

## When unsure

Point to the site rather than improvising. Hostile or ambiguous replies get a
short, polite pointer, or nothing.

## Verification before you record anything

Record a connection only when the name appears in LinkedIn's Sent
invitations. Record a message only when the sent bubble is present on a fresh
reopen of the thread. Record a reply only when it shows under the post.
Unverified means not logged.
