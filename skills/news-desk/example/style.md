# Style — citycycle.example

- **Objective and specific.** What happened, when, where, how much; who
  announced it. No opinion, no hype.
- **Headline of our own**, under 80 characters. Never the source's title.
- **300-500 words**: the news, the key facts, what it means for riders, and
  the context a reader needs. Link the primary source.
- **Never** state a number, date or quote we could not check against the
  primary source.
- End with: "Follow citycycle.example for the next update."
